# Comparison dimensions

User-specified dimensions take priority. Choose only defaults that answer the question.

## Normalize before comparing

- Terms and construct definitions
- Units and denominators
- Baselines and controls
- Sample identity, quality, and quantity
- Experimental, clinical, or computational conditions
- Measurement methods and detection limits
- Statistical definitions and aggregation
- Model or theorem assumptions
- Applicable regime and time scale

## Useful axes by task

- Mechanism: proposed pathway, active component, intermediate, causal support, alternative explanation.
- Method: inputs, procedure, instrumentation, parameters, validation, reproducibility, cost.
- Performance: metric definition, normalization, test conditions, baseline, variance, stability.
- Theory: assumptions, formal statement, proof or derivation, regime, limiting case, validation.
- Clinical: population, intervention or exposure, comparator, endpoint, effect size, bias, external validity.
- Materials and catalysis: composition, structure, active site, preparation, characterization, reaction conditions, transport, durability.

## Matrix labels

- `direct`: definitions and conditions are sufficiently aligned.
- `qualitative`: direction or mechanism can be compared, but values cannot.
- `not_comparable`: a material mismatch blocks comparison.
- `missing`: the source does not report the needed field.

Never fill a missing cell by inference. Summarize agreements, differences, conflicts, and gaps after the matrix, and explain which condition causes a comparability limit.
